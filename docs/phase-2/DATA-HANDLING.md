
## Personal emails (decision)

Work and personal emails are both offered; personal emails are switched per region.

Starting rule (proposal): personal emails are off in every region until counsel and the provider licence clear that region. Every returned email is labelled work or personal.

Why the caution (for counsel to confirm): a personal email is more clearly personal data than a work address, so the balancing test behind legitimate interest is harder; rules on emailing individuals are stricter in several regions.

Provider checks: documented source and lawful basis for personal emails; suppression and opt-out coverage; whether the licence allows passing them to our users; accuracy of the work-or-personal label.

Guardrails: label every email; acceptable-use rule against bulk unsolicited outreach to personal emails; personal emails left out of exports where off; suppression applies to both types; the audit entry records the region rule applied.

Tests: where personal emails are off, no tool, export or adapter output returns one; every email carries a label; suppression covers both types.
